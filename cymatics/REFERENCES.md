# References used for the scientific model

1. **NCBI Bookshelf — DNA: Alternative Conformations and Biology**. Canonical B-DNA: about 10.5 bp/turn, 3.4 Å rise per residue, ~20 Å diameter.
   https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK6545/

2. **Young et al. (2024), DNA simulation benchmarks revealed with the accumulation of high-resolution structures.** Average rigid-body parameters for ten unique dinucleotide steps.
   https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11297015/

3. **Olson et al. / sequence-dependent DNA structure literature.** Base-pair steps are described by six parameters: tilt, roll, twist, shift, slide, rise.
   https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC21613/

4. **Cymatics / Chladni-mode mathematical background.** Chladni figures arise from standing-wave modes; real frequencies depend on the physical resonator.
   https://evoluteur.github.io/cymatics/
